    How jobs are checked

    Scope

    US locations only. AI/ML, Data and Software roles: a tech word in the title, or strong tech terms in the job description. First seen in the last 24 hours or 2 days.

    Work authorization

    Citizenship no US citizenship, green card, clearance or ITAR "U.S. person". CPT / F-1 not rejected, no "permanent" authorization. Sponsorship no "no sponsorship now or in the future" line.

    Education

    Degree master's students can apply (not undergraduate-only or PhD-only). Graduation any stated window includes May 2028.

    Only clean matches

    A job is listed only when every check passes. Hidden: any failed check, "no sponsorship now or in the future", "bachelor's preferred", readers that disagree, and postings whose description could not be read. Open Details on a job to see the sentence behind each check.
